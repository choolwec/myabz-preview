What’s new

  • Mobile money transfers Send to Airtel, MTN or Zamtel. The network is worked out from the number.
  • Airtime & data Top up any network, or buy a bundle, straight from your account.
  • Foreign exchange See today’s rates and buy or sell currency without going into a branch.
  • ZRA, NAPSA and NHIMA Statutory payments alongside the usual bills.
  • Built for a phone Checked on every screen size from 320px up. Most customers are on a small Android.
  • Branch & agent finder Branches plus the Kazang and 543 Konse Konse agent network.
  • Clearer payments You always see the recipient’s name and the fee before you authorise.
  • First-login experience For customers the contact centre has just registered.

Things to look at

Worth clicking through specifically: Transfer → To a mobile money wallet (type 0966410228 and watch the network and recipient name fill themselves in), Templates (pick one and it fills everything except the amount), and Settings → Language. Switch to the business profile from the top-left to see payment approvals.

Telling us what to change

Note the screen name and what felt wrong — wording, order of fields, anything missing or confusing. Screenshots help. Nothing here is final; it is much cheaper to change now than after the backend is built.
